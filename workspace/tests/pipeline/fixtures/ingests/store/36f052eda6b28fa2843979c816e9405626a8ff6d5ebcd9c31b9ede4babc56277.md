# Northern Reach Observatory

**Internal note - Skerrivore Point enquiry**

Dr Helena Marsh has been asked to prepare a summary of the observatory's
records for the period 13 to 15 March 1994. The plate register, the duty log
and the seeing conditions sheet are all held in the director's cabinet and
none of them has been removed from the building.

Coastal Air Defence Command (CADC) has acknowledged our letter of 8 April and
has undertaken to say by the end of the month whether the Skerrivore Point
returns were retained. No undertaking has been given about the tapes.

The enquiry papers are filed under reference NRO/1994/17.

Correspondence on this matter should be directed to Marion Kilbride, who is
keeping the file.
