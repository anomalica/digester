# Weaponized - Immaculate Constellation Part One (Excerpt)

This is an interview conducted by Jeremy Corbell and George Knapp with the author of the Immaculate Constellation report, identified as Brown.

**Corbell:**
Is it ironic you have to put your face out with us to protect yourself at that at this point? That's what we're doing, right?

**Brown:**
Yeah, that's what we're doing. This is absolutely what I did not want to do.

**Knapp:**
You've worked in secret programs. You've got clearances. You've made oaths that you would keep secrets. Let's talk about secrecy and the UAP topic. Shouldn't it be secret to some degree? I mean, we have a race for the technology to try to understand it. We have adversary nations who'd love to get it ahead of us. Why not keep this stuff secret?

**Brown:**
Well, if it was just technology that we are worried about protecting and maintaining a military advantage, you know, there's strong arguments to be made and have successfully been made for decades in this country that the American leadership and American people seem to be finding acceptable to be left in the dark about. This isn't just technology. This isn't something somebody came up with in a lab and it's sensitive because once the cat's out of the bag, anybody can do it. Fundamentally, this comes from and is secondary to what we've been told is the biggest question which is: is humanity alone? The answer is no. And the secrecy that has been defended is at the cost of, in my mind, human dignity, freedom and progress. And it is no longer permissible or acceptable in my mind to continue this course to deprive another generation of not just Americans but humanity of their birthright to know who they are, where they came from, and what's with us.

**Knapp:**
Could you give us a general description of the size of the secrecy apparatus?

**Brown:**
Simply put, it's a parallel reality. The secrecy apparatus is all pervasive. It is not just in the intelligence community governing their world. It's not just in the military protecting their operations. It has been allowed to corrupt and infest every aspect of our society to serve the interests and whims of a very elite select few.

**Knapp:**
Like a business cabal? International corporations that have no loyalty to any nation, let alone this one?

**Brown:**
That's a large part of it. I think we have to go above that too to start to explain the degree of the level of deception and the level of commitment they've had to maintaining that deception. It's above just monetary gain and power. They're afraid.

**Corbell:**
What are the legal implications of what you're about to talk to us today? Tell me the gravity right now. What are you facing?

**Brown:**
My hope is that the stakes are not paid out. But they are life imprisonment and the possibility of execution.

**Knapp:**
You got a degree in international affairs, came out to DC, started working in a small think tank. From there you applied to and were accepted to an intelligence agency. Ended up taking a job at the Pentagon.

**Brown:**
Yeah, I had been cleared by DIA for a top secret SCI clearance.

**Knapp:**
You get to see firsthand all this different information that comes in from all over, different platforms. You're exposed to that and you get an idea of just how big that apparatus is.

**Brown:**
Yes. Absolutely. UFOs and aliens aside, it's a different world. When you are exposed to that information and learning over time, not just from the intelligence, but the experiences of the people around you. Not everything that's true and has happened in the world is written down on paper.

**Corbell:**
I mean, you're given a lot of roles and responsibilities. It gives you access to very sensitive information. You have access to very critical information through the United States government. They're separate from the UFOs and the alien topic. For the world, for the US and the world.

**Brown:**
Yes.

**Knapp:**
Okay. You're there for a while. You do that for how many years?

**Brown:**
I was in there a little over four years, but I was in the Pentagon a little over five because I moved out of there briefly into the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence, a smaller office in there.
